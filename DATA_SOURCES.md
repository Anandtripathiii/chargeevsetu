## State-wise public EV charging stations (Ministry of Power inputs, PIB annexure)

- **Type:** Real
- **Source:** https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/aug/doc202581598101.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-23 (data as of Aug 2025)
- **Licence:** Government of India (GODL / public record)
- **Records:** 36
- **Cleaning:** Parsed from PDF; state names normalised; row total checked against printed total (29,277).
- **Known limitations:** Counts stations, not connectors. Telangana/Andhra split as reported.

## OpenStreetMap charging stations (amenity=charging_station)

- **Type:** Real
- **Source:** https://overpass-api.de / maps.mail.ru Overpass mirror
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-23
- **Licence:** ODbL 1.0
- **Records:** 608
- **Cleaning:** Nodes/ways/relations reduced to a point (way centre); kept operator, sockets, capacity, power tags.
- **Known limitations:** Volunteer-mapped; coverage varies by city. Many operator stations are not yet in OSM.

## District & state boundaries (Census 2011 districts, DataMeet)

- **Type:** Real
- **Source:** https://github.com/datameet/maps/tree/master/Districts/Census_2011
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-23
- **Licence:** CC BY 2.5 IN (DataMeet)
- **Records:** 640
- **Cleaning:** Telangana districts split from Andhra Pradesh; Leh & Kargil moved to Ladakh; states dissolved from districts; geometry simplified (states ~1 km, districts ~500 m).

## Census of India 2011 district tables (population, households, urban households, vehicle ownership)

- **Type:** Real
- **Source:** https://github.com/nishusharma1608/India-Census-2011-Analysis (Census 2011 PCA/HH tables)
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-23
- **Licence:** Government of India open data
- **Records:** 640
- **Cleaning:** Joined on census district code; urban share = urban households / households.
- **Known limitations:** 2026 population = 2011 x 1.206 (UN WPP national growth) for every district -> marked Estimated.

## Merged real charger points (OSM + OCM, de-duplicated)

- **Type:** Real
- **Source:** derived in pipeline/build.py
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-23
- **Licence:** n/a
- **Records:** 606
- **Cleaning:** OSM 608; OCM duplicates merged 0; OCM new 0. Rule: <=50 m apart and same/unknown operator or name similarity > 0.5 -> one charger with both sources listed. Dropped 2 points outside India's district boundaries (offshore / across the border).
- **Known limitations:** Mapped points cover only part of the official total; see completeness per state.

## Modelled charger locations (to reach official totals)

- **Type:** Synthetic
- **Source:** generated in pipeline/build.py
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-23
- **Licence:** n/a
- **Records:** 52120
- **Cleaning:** Per district: derived official total minus mapped real points; positions sampled from WorldPop cells with probability ~ population^1.5 (seed 42); DC share 31.4% from Lok Sabha USQ 453.
- **Known limitations:** Positions are statistical placeholders, not real stations. Used only so distance/coverage metrics reflect the official charger count; switch them off on the map to see mapped points only.
